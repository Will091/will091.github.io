Bio.

JaceBen is a Nigerian artist, songwriter, and producer driven by one thing: turning real life into sound.

His music lives between emotion, ambition, pain, love, and the relentless pursuit of something bigger. With a sound that refuses to be boxed into one genre, JaceBen is building more than a catalogue — he’s building a world, one record at a time.
